# Metric definitions

Demo data for a made-up product, the Synthetic Readmissions Feed. One row per metric. A move is a week-over-week change larger than the threshold.

| Metric | Definition | Grain | Owner | Move threshold (%) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Readmission rate, 30 days | Share of discharges with a return inside 30 days | weekly | Priya Nair | 10 |
| Active consumers | Distinct consumers who ran at least one query | weekly | Ana Reyes | 15 |
| Rows loaded | Rows loaded into the feed in the week | weekly | Ana Reyes | 20 |
| Median query seconds | Median run time of consumer queries | weekly | Marcus Webb | 25 |
